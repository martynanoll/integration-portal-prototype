Rollout procedure

Details how new gym locations will be activated after the pilot. Review and accept the procedure. You may propose changes if your rollout differs.

Standard rollout steps

EGYM standard
Partner service ▾
  1. The gym contacts its membership management partner to request the integration.
  2. The partner explains the integration and advises the gym on its requirements.
  3. The partner sends the gym's name and address to the integration team.
  4. The integration team checks whether the gym's data needs cleaning before activation.
  5. The integration team and partner review an extract of the member data and agree on any necessary actions.
  6. The partner configures the integration and product bookings for the gym.
  7. The integration team provides the partner with the credentials needed to connect.
  8. The partner, gym and integration team agree on an activation date and time.
  9. The integration team activates the agreed services and shares the product configuration details with the partner.
  10. The partner and gym test a user account, product booking and member identification together.
  11. If needed, transfer existing members in bulk, then disable account creation in the trainer app.
